Greater-Than in GPT-2 Small: Circuit Analysis & Steering

Independent Mechanistic Interpretability Project
2026

Given "The war started in 1732 and ended in 17", GPT-2 small puts most of its probability on years greater than 32. I replicated the circuit analysis from Hanna, Liu & Variengien (2023) to find which heads implement this comparison, then studied where the model fails at temperature 0 and whether a steering vector can fix it.

  • Circuit analysis: mean-ablation attribution recovered 6 of the paper's 8 circuit heads, with head 9.1 dominant. Patching just the top-8 heads from corrupted prompts drops accuracy from 1.00 to 0.55, while 8 random heads have no effect.
  • Failure analysis: on 1,000 prompts, 74 fail, and 61 of those simply copy the start year. Failures concentrate in years that tokenize as a single token (e.g. ' 365'), where the circuit has no separate YY token to attend to.
  • Steering: a vector built by mapping the target-minus-model distribution back into the residual stream with a ridge pseudoinverse fixes 82% of held-out failures, breaks only 1.5% of correct prompts, and raises overall accuracy from 0.926 to 0.972.
  • Caveat: the vector learns a general "later year" direction rather than "greater than this year": it still scores well when the start year is removed, and piles probability onto 99.

Gradient Boosting on Company Financial Statements

Independent Project
2026

An XGBoost regression pipeline trained on five years (2014–2018) of US company fundamentals: about 22,000 company-year rows across roughly 5,000 tickers, each with 200+ income-statement, balance-sheet, cash-flow and valuation indicators. The model predicts a company's revenue growth from the rest of its fundamentals.

  • Built a scikit-learn pipeline (ColumnTransformer) with mean imputation for numeric features and one-hot encoding for categorical fields such as sector, then excluded price-return columns from the features to avoid leaking outcomes.
  • Explored the feature space with a covariance heatmap of standardized indicators, which shows the expected blocks of highly redundant metrics (margins, per-share values, multi-year growth rates).
  • Ran an overfitting check by sweeping the number of boosting rounds. Training MSE collapses toward zero while validation MSE stays flat, and the analysis traced this to a heavy-tailed target (median growth about 6%, but a maximum above 40,000×) that squared error is very sensitive to.
